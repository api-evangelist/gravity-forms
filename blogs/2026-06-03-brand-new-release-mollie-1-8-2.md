Gravity Forms Mollie Add-On 1.8.2 introduces accessibility improvements and a new filter for customizing return URLs, while also preventing the Mollie field from being placed in repeaters or duplicated within the form editor. The update enhances compatibility with recent Gravity Forms versions and supports European and international payment methods including iDEAL and Bancontact.
